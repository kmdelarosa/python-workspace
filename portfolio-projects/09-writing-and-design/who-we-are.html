Who We Are

A little about me

Someone who genuinely likes understanding hard things — and making them easier for everyone else.

I collaborate with companies to understand their products deeply — then translate that understanding into documentation and visual content that actually works for the people who need to use them.

My process starts with the product and the audience. I ask what users need to know, what they're likely confused about, and what would make them confident. Then I build content around those answers — whether that's a structured API reference, an onboarding guide, or a visual explainer.

I work across technical writing and visual design because the two reinforce each other. A well-structured doc becomes more powerful with the right diagram. A visual asset lands harder when the copy behind it is precise.
