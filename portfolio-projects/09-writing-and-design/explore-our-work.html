How I work & what I make

I engage with product understanding first, then audience positioning — translating business value into content that's accessible, accurate, and built to be used.

How I work

01

Product understanding

I start by getting deep into the product — how it works, what it does, and what makes it technically distinct. No good content comes from a surface-level read of a one-pager.

02

Target audience positioning

Who needs this content? What do they already know, and where do they get stuck? I map that gap and build everything around closing it — whether the audience is a developer or a CFO.

03

Translating business value into accessible content

I bridge the gap between what a product can do and what users need to understand — crafting written and visual assets that make complex things feel clear and worth trusting.
